About Bolbala Trust

People first.
Purpose always.

Bolbala Trust is a charitable organization based in Rajkot, Gujarat. Its work brings together community members and volunteers to support education, healthcare, food assistance, animal welfare and other community needs.

The Trust's public website describes programmes ranging from Jiv Daya and educational support to healthcare initiatives, food service, skill development and environmental care.

Our story

Care that starts with people.

Bolbala Trust is a charitable organization based in Rajkot, Gujarat. Its public website describes a commitment to serving humanity through education, healthcare, community support, animal welfare and sustainable development.

What guides us

Dignity, empathy and action.

The Trust presents its work as community service rooted in public participation. Its current website highlights Rajkot-focused initiatives and describes the guiding belief as “Serving humanity is serving God.”

✓Respect every life
Treat people and animals with care and dignity.
✓Work with communities
Listen, collaborate and respond to real needs.
✓Act responsibly
Communicate clearly and use resources thoughtfully.
Our commitment

Serving humanity is serving God.

The Trust's published programme areas include Jiv Daya, Shiksha Sahayata, Swasthya Seva, Samudayik Vikas, Mahila Shakti, Prakriti Raksha and Kaushal Vikas. Programme descriptions and results should be kept current by the Trust.
